## Overview

This project assessed how two existing campus office buildings in Hong Kong could reduce operational carbon emissions by 50% by 2035 while maintaining practical investment pathways.

## Method

EnergyPlus simulation was integrated with life-cycle cost analysis for two campus office buildings. Strategies addressing occupant demand, renewable generation, lighting, windows, and mechanical ventilation and air-conditioning systems were evaluated individually and in combined retrofit packages.

## Key findings

The combined strategies reduced operational carbon emissions by 50.1% and 50.5% in the two case-study buildings. Chiller upgrades delivered the largest energy savings, followed by LED lighting and renewable-energy systems. Shallow retrofits and LED systems offered the shortest payback periods, while envelope measures requiring exterior scaffolding generally had longer paybacks.
