Background
======
Conventional glare assessment can be computationally expensive when it is applied across many viewpoints and time steps. This ongoing project explores data-driven methods for identifying uncomfortable visual conditions more efficiently while retaining interpretable performance indicators.

Method
======
The study investigates image-based and simulation-informed learning workflows for glare detection. Additional methodological details and results will be added as the research progresses.
