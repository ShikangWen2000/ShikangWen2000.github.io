Background
======
Urban-scale energy forecasting supports retrofit planning, infrastructure decisions, and low-carbon development. The challenge is to represent diverse building characteristics and operating patterns while maintaining a workflow that can scale across large building stocks.

Method
======
This ongoing project examines data preparation, building-feature representation, and machine-learning approaches for forecasting energy demand at urban scale. Additional methodological details and results will be added as the research progresses.
